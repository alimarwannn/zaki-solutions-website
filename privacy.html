Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Who we are

Zaki Solutions ("Zaki", "we") provides an AI assistant and operations dashboard that businesses in Egypt (restaurants, cafés, clinics, salons, venues and other service businesses) use to handle bookings, orders, payments, receipts and customer messages. Zaki answers customers on WhatsApp, Instagram Direct and the business's table and QR ordering pages.

We process customer data on behalf of the business the customer is talking to. That business decides how its customer records are used; Zaki Solutions operates the service for it.

One exception is shared across businesses: when you visit another business that uses Zaki, its staff may see a general label about you, such as "VIP elsewhere in the Zaki network" or "High spender elsewhere". That label never names the other business and never shows amounts or what you ordered.

What data we collect

  • Contact details: name and phone number. On Instagram, the account ID Instagram shares with the business (not your username).
  • Messages: the content of messages you send to a business that uses Zaki (WhatsApp, Instagram Direct or the ordering page) and Zaki's replies. On WhatsApp this includes voice notes, which are transcribed to text unless the business has turned this off.
  • How to address you: Zaki may infer from the wording of your messages whether to address you in the masculine or feminine form, so its Arabic replies are grammatically correct.
  • Bookings and orders: dates, times, party size, items ordered, special requests, dietary or allergy notes you share, and booking or order status.
  • Payments and receipts: amounts, payment method, payment confirmations, receipts, and screenshots you send as proof of a transfer (for example InstaPay). Card payments are processed by the payment provider; we do not store card numbers.
  • Booking verification (only where the business requires it): a photo of your ID and your Instagram or Facebook handle, so the business can confirm a booking. ID photos are deleted after your visit.
  • Guest account (optional): if you sign in on a business's ordering page (for example with Google or Facebook), your email address, sign-in identifiers and saved preferences, such as dietary preferences. A guest account works across the businesses that use Zaki.
  • Feedback: ratings and comments you choose to leave.

How we use it

  • To answer your messages and create, change or cancel your bookings and orders.
  • To send messages you asked for or that relate to an active booking or order: confirmations, reminders, receipts and payment updates.
  • To send messages on behalf of the business after a visit: a request to rate your visit, an occasional "we miss you" message if you haven't visited for a while, and offers the business announces. You can stop these at any time (see Opting out).
  • To give the business its dashboard: live bookings, orders, customer history and reports.
  • To keep the service secure and working, and to fix problems.

We do not sell or rent personal data, we do not share it with advertising networks, and we do not use it for ad targeting.

Instagram and WhatsApp

Zaki uses Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform and the Instagram Messaging API. When you message a business on Instagram or WhatsApp, Meta shares that message and your Instagram account ID with the business's Zaki assistant so it can reply. On Instagram, Zaki answers your questions (for example about the menu, opening hours or allergens) and points you to the business's WhatsApp for bookings; it does not take bookings or orders on Instagram. We use your Instagram messages only to reply to you on behalf of that business. Your use of WhatsApp and Instagram is also covered by Meta's own privacy policies.

Service providers

We use these providers to run the service. Each receives only what it needs:

  • Meta (WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook): message delivery, and optional Facebook sign-in on ordering pages where offered.
  • Anthropic: the AI model that writes Zaki's replies.
  • Supabase: database and file storage, for example payment screenshots and ID photos (EU).
  • Railway: application hosting (EU).
  • OpenAI: transcription of WhatsApp voice notes, unless the business has turned it off.
  • Google: optional sign-in on ordering pages, and spreadsheet copies of bookings for the business, where turned on.
  • Paymob: card payments, where the business has turned them on.
  • Resend: email delivery, for example emailed receipts.
  • Sentry: error monitoring.

Opting out

Reply STOP on WhatsApp to stop "we miss you" messages, offers and other promotional messages. You may still receive messages about an active booking, order, payment or receipt.

Data retention

We keep records for as long as the business needs them for its bookings, orders, support, reporting and legal obligations, or until deletion is requested and legally possible.

Your rights and deleting your data

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data. See how to request deletion, or email info@zaki-solutions.com.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access to business dashboards is restricted by login and role, and payment card details stay with the payment provider.

Changes
