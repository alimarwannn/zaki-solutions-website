Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Who we are

Zaki Solutions ("Zaki", "we") provides an AI assistant and operations dashboard that businesses in Egypt (restaurants, cafés, clinics, salons, venues and other service businesses) use to handle bookings, orders, payments, receipts and customer messages. Zaki answers customers on WhatsApp, Instagram Direct and the business's table and QR ordering pages.

We process customer data on behalf of the business the customer is talking to. That business decides how its customer records are used; Zaki Solutions operates the service for it.

What data we collect

  • Contact details: name, phone number, and for Instagram, your Instagram username and the account ID Instagram shares with the business.
  • Messages: the content of messages you send to a business that uses Zaki (WhatsApp, Instagram Direct or the ordering page), including voice notes where the business has turned on voice transcription, and Zaki's replies.
  • Bookings and orders: dates, times, party size, items ordered, special requests, dietary or allergy notes you share, and booking or order status.
  • Payments and receipts: amounts, payment method, payment confirmations and receipts. Card payments are processed by the payment provider; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Guest account (optional): if you sign in on a business's ordering page (for example with Google), your email address, sign-in identifiers and saved preferences.
  • Feedback: ratings and comments you choose to leave.

How we use it

  • To answer your messages and create, change or cancel your bookings and orders.
  • To send messages you asked for or that relate to an active booking or order: confirmations, reminders, receipts and payment updates.
  • To give the business its dashboard: live bookings, orders, customer history and reports.
  • To keep the service secure and working, and to fix problems.

We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Instagram and WhatsApp

Zaki uses Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform and the Instagram Messaging API. When you message a business on Instagram or WhatsApp, Meta shares that message and your public profile details (such as your username) with the business's Zaki assistant so it can reply. We use these only to reply to you and to handle your booking or order with that business. Your use of WhatsApp and Instagram is also covered by Meta's own privacy policies.

Service providers

We use these providers to run the service. Each receives only what it needs:

  • Meta (WhatsApp, Instagram): message delivery.
  • Anthropic: the AI model that writes Zaki's replies.
  • Supabase: database hosting (EU).
  • Railway: application hosting (EU).
  • OpenAI: voice-note transcription, where the business has turned it on.
  • Google: optional sign-in on ordering pages, and spreadsheet copies of bookings for the business, where turned on.
  • Paymob: card payments, where the business has turned them on.
  • Resend: email delivery, for example emailed receipts.
  • Sentry: error monitoring.

Opting out

Reply STOP on WhatsApp to stop promotional messages. You may still receive messages about an active booking, order, payment or receipt.

Data retention

We keep records for as long as the business needs them for its bookings, orders, support, reporting and legal obligations, or until deletion is requested and legally possible.

Your rights and deleting your data

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data. See how to request deletion, or email info@zaki-solutions.com.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access to business dashboards is restricted by login and role, and payment card details stay with the payment provider.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and show the new date at the top.
